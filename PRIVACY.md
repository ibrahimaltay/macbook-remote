# LazyRemote Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

The canonical copy of this policy is at https://ibrahimaltay.github.io/lazy-remote-for-desktop/privacy.html.

LazyRemote does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Bluetooth.** The iPhone app uses Bluetooth only to connect directly to your own Mac
  and send remote-control input (button presses, pointer movement, text). This traffic
  is end-to-end encrypted and never leaves your devices or passes through any server.
- **On-device storage.** Pairing keys are stored in the Keychain, and app preferences
  are stored locally on your device. They are never transmitted to us.
- **Children.** The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
  children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
